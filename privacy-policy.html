🔒 Privacy Policy & Data Deletion Instructions

How your data is handled in the Synthesis application

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Synthesis ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This document describes how your information is handled and how you can request or perform data deletion when using the Synthesis mobile application (the "App").

2. Data Collection and Usage

Synthesis is designed as an offline-first industrial management application for production reporting, asphalt mixture statistics, fuel refills, and laboratory mixture tracking.

  • Account & Authentication: You may create an account using Email/Password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple. When using social sign-in, we receive only your email address, display name, and a unique identifier from the respective provider. We do not access your Google or Apple account data beyond what is needed for authentication.
  • Cloud Sync (Firebase): If you are signed in, your production data (reports, mixtures, materials, fuel logs, sites) is securely synchronized to Google Cloud Firestore under your authenticated user account. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Each user can only access their own data — enforced by server-side security rules.
  • Offline Mode: You can opt to use the App in a strictly Offline Mode from the Settings. In Offline Mode, no data is synced, and features relying on Cloud services (like AI OCR) are disabled, keeping all operations entirely local.
  • Local Storage: Production reports, mixture recipes, material quantities, fuel logs, and site information are stored locally on your device using a secure, encrypted SQLite database.
  • Biometric Data: If you enable App Lock, biometric authentication (Fingerprint or Face ID) is processed entirely on-device by your Operating System's native security APIs. We do not collect, transmit, or store your biometric data.
  • AI-Powered OCR (Bring-Your-Own-Key): The App supports document scanning via third-party AI services: Google Gemini, OpenAI (GPT-4o), Anthropic (Claude), and DeepSeek. The App follows a Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) model — you provide your own API key, and image data is transmitted directly from your device to the AI provider you choose. Photos are processed ephemerally for text extraction only and are not stored by us or by the AI providers under their API terms of service. You can review each provider's data handling policy independently.
  • On-Device OCR (Google ML Kit): As an alternative to cloud AI, the App offers fully offline text recognition using Google ML Kit, which runs entirely on your device. No image data leaves your phone when using this mode.
  • Camera and Storage Access: The App may request access to your device camera solely for scanning production documents. Storage access is requested only for exporting reports (PDF/Excel files). Camera hardware is declared as optional — the App works on devices without a camera.
  • App Integrity (Firebase App Check): To protect our backend services from abuse, we use Firebase App Check with Android Play Integrity. This verifies that requests originate from a genuine installation of the App. No personal data is collected by this process.

3. Data Protection and Third-Party Sharing

  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or any third-party tracking or advertising SDK. We do not track your activity across apps or websites.
  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.
  • Data Transfers: Your data remains on your local device and, if signed in, on Google Cloud Firestore (secured under your account). Data is only transferred externally when you explicitly: (a) use AI OCR with your own API key, (b) export a PDF/Excel report, or (c) share a backup file.

Third-Party Services Used

The App integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check) — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google ML Kit (On-device text recognition) — processed locally, no data transmitted
  • Google Gemini API (Cloud AI OCR, if selected) — Gemini API Terms
  • OpenAI API (Cloud AI OCR, if selected) — OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic API (Cloud AI OCR, if selected) — Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • DeepSeek API (Cloud AI OCR, if selected) — DeepSeek Privacy Policy

4. User Data Deletion Instructions

Since Synthesis stores your data locally on your mobile device, you have total control over your data deletion:

A. In-App Deletion (Full Wipe)

  • Account Deletion: You have the right to permanently delete your account directly from the App's Account Settings. Performing an account deletion triggers a Full Wipe, which securely and irreversibly erases all your data from both your local device (SQLite databases) and our Cloud servers (Firebase).
  • Clear App Cache/Data: Alternatively, you can clear app data via Android Device Settings → Apps → Synthesis → Storage → Clear Data. This will delete all local data, but it will not delete your Cloud account. To completely erase your presence, please use the in-app Account Deletion feature.

B. Request Data Deletion Assistance

  • Email Request: Send an email to kwstas147@gmail.com with the subject line "Synthesis Data Deletion Request".
  • Processing: We will guide you on confirming full local and cloud deletion or assist with any inquiries within 48 hours.

5. Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption at Rest: Authentication tokens and API keys are stored using Android Encrypted Shared Preferences / KeyStore.
  • Encryption in Transit: All network communications use HTTPS/TLS exclusively. Cleartext HTTP traffic is blocked at the system level via Android Network Security Configuration.
  • Access Control: Cloud Firestore security rules enforce strict per-user data isolation — users can only read and write their own data.
  • App Integrity: Firebase App Check with Play Integrity validates that API requests originate from a genuine app installation.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for professional use and does not knowingly target or collect personal information from children under 13.
